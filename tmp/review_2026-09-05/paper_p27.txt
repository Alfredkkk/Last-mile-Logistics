4.5. Numerical Study
In this subsection, we numerically evaluate the zoning-based coordination policy based on parameters
calibrated using the yellow taxi data from New York City (NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission,
2023). Specifically, we consider a service region with areaA = 7.68×7.68 = 59km2, which is the size of
the Manhattan borough. We setv = 7.1 mph = 0.19 km/minute (NYC Department of Transportation,
2018) andα= 0.25. Based on the trip record, we setλ·t0 = 100requests (Ata et al., 2019). According
to the observed fare structure and statistics, the revenue rates from passenger rides and package
delivery arert = $5.5/km of ride time andrp = $0.8 per package ($20/hour for 25 packages per hour),
respectively (Schneider, 2023; A Martínez, 2023). We also examine cases with a higher ride fare
rt = $6/km, considering surge prices. Because the number of packages to be delivered varies according
to the vehicle capacity, we test different values ofn that can be as large as 60. More details about the
parameter calibration are presented in Appendix G.
