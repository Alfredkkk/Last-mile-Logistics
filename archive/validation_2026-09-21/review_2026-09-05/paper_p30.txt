Our results suggest that the revenue-maximizing zoning policy has a simple structure with three
different levels of flexibility. Specifically, in the regimes with low package volumes, coordinating
package delivery and on-demand rides is preferred over pure package delivery. When the package
volume is lower than a threshold, the optimal coordination policy reduces to a flexible switching policy.
