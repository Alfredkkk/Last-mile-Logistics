4.2. Approximation Function for the Inter-Zone Delivery Distance L3(i)
The key analytical difficulty of assessing the general zoning policy lies in the inter-zone delivery
distance that is a function of the number of remaining zones to be visited. Formally, the expected
inter-zone delivery distance under the zoning-based coordination policy withN zones{Z1,...,Zn}is
L3(i) = ER [d(Zi,Z−R,i)], 1≤i≤N−1, (5)
whereR ={Z1,...,Zi}is the random visiting sequence ofi zones following the coordination policy,

of the closest distance, we assume it is broken randomly), andd(Zi,Z−R,i) is the distance between a
random package location inZi and a random package location inZ−R,i.
